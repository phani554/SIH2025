NOTICE INVITING TENDER
Tender No: KMRL/PROC/2025/T-219
Date of issue: 15 September 2025
Subject: Supply, installation and commissioning of IP CCTV cameras and video analytics at 10 stations

Kochi Metro Rail Limited invites sealed bids from eligible vendors for upgrading the CCTV system at 10 stations, including Aluva, Kalamassery, Edappally, MG Road and Vyttila.
Estimated cost: Rs. 4.2 crore. Earnest Money Deposit: Rs. 8,40,000.
Last date for bid submission: 10 October 2025, 3:00 PM. Pre-bid meeting: 25 September 2025 at KMRL head office.
Bidders must have completed at least two similar metro or railway CCTV projects in the last five years.
