OFFICE OF THE COMMISSIONER OF METRO RAIL SAFETY, SOUTHERN CIRCLE, BENGALURU
Ref: CMRS/SC/KMRL/2025/41
Date: 8 September 2025
To: Managing Director, Kochi Metro Rail Limited

Subject: Statutory inspection of the Vyttila to SN Junction extension

The statutory inspection of the Vyttila to SN Junction section was carried out on 4 and 5 September 2025.
Authorisation to open the section for passenger service is granted subject to the conditions below.

Condition 1: The speed restriction of 25 km/h on the curve near Petta shall remain until the rail lubrication system is commissioned.
Condition 2: Fire detection and suppression systems at SN Junction station shall be tested in the presence of the fire department and a compliance report submitted within 30 days.
Condition 3: Emergency evacuation walkways along the viaduct shall be inspected every month for the first six months of operation.

Non-compliance with any condition may lead to withdrawal of the authorisation.
