SAFETY CIRCULAR No. SC/14/2025
Date: 3 September 2025
Issued by: Chief Safety Officer, KMRL
Subject: Immediate inspection of platform screen door sensors at Edappally and Palarivattom stations

Following an incident on 1 September 2025 at Edappally station where a platform screen door closed while a passenger was boarding, all station controllers must carry out a full inspection of door obstruction sensors within 48 hours.
The incident has been reported to the Commissioner of Metro Rail Safety (CMRS) as required.

Actions required:
1. Station controllers at Edappally and Palarivattom to test every door obstruction sensor and log results by 5 September 2025.
2. Maintenance department to replace any faulty sensor units immediately.
3. Operations to run trains with manual door confirmation at the two stations until the inspection is complete.

Non-compliance will be treated as a safety violation.
