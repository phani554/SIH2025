HR NOTICE No. HR/2025/88
Date: 22 September 2025
Subject: Mandatory customer service and Malayalam signage training for station staff

All customer relations staff and station controllers must attend a one-day training on passenger communication, accessibility assistance and the updated Malayalam and English signage standards.
Batches will run at the KMRL Training Centre, Muttom, from 6 October to 17 October 2025.
Staff must register through their reporting officer by 30 September 2025. Attendance will be recorded and is linked to the annual appraisal.
