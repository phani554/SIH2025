AMENDMENT No. 2 TO CONTRACT KMRL/ADMIN/HK/2023/07
Housekeeping services at stations: M/s Clean Kerala Facility Services Pvt Ltd

This amendment is made on 19 September 2025 between Kochi Metro Rail Limited and M/s Clean Kerala Facility Services Pvt Ltd.

Clause 4.2 (Contract period) is amended: the contract is extended by twelve months, to 31 March 2027.

Clause 7.1 (Rates) is amended: the monthly charge per station is revised from Rs. 2,10,000 to Rs. 2,28,000 with effect from 1 October 2025, to reflect the revised minimum wages notified by the Government of Kerala.

Clause 11 (Penalties) is amended: a penalty of Rs. 5,000 per instance applies if a station toilet is found unclean during a surprise inspection.

All other terms of the original contract remain unchanged. Any dispute shall be resolved by arbitration in Kochi.
